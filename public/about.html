MMM OS

Private operator dashboard

MMM OS is a private, single-operator dashboard application for managing AI-assisted content production and tracking the resulting channel performance. It is used internally to run several content engines — including NextWave, SRV Farsi, SRV English, and AI Creation Studio — and to monitor their YouTube channel analytics in one place.

MMM OS is not a public product and is not open for third-party registration. Access is restricted to its authorised operator.

What MMM OS Does

  • Tracks YouTube channel and video analytics for its connected content engines
  • Generates and manages AI-assisted video content packages, with every publish step requiring explicit operator review and approval before anything is uploaded
  • Tracks financial figures the operator enters manually, for personal/business tracking purposes
