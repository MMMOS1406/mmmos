Waiting to invest costs more than most people think. Say you invest $250 every month, assuming an average 6 percent annual return. If you start today, that portfolio grows to about $115,510 after 20 years. But if you wait four years before you start, contributing the same $250 a month for the remaining 16 years, you end up with only about $80,273. That four year delay costs you $35,237. Start this month, not next year.
