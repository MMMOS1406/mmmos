Every year you wait to invest gets more expensive. Say you invest $350 every month, assuming an average 7 percent annual return. If you start today, that portfolio grows to about $426,990 after 30 years. But if you wait ten years before you start, contributing the same $350 a month for the remaining 20 years, you end up with only about $182,324. That ten year delay costs you $244,666. Start now, not later.
